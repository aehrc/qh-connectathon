# FHIR in Qld Connectathon 2026

## What is the FHIR in Queensland Health Connectathon?

The FHIR in Queensland Health Connectathon is a collaborative, hands-on event where Queensland Health staff, clinicians, analysts, researchers, architects, developers and interoperability specialists come together to explore how HL7® FHIR® can be used to solve real-world healthcare challenges.

Unlike a conference or training course, a Connectathon is a working event. Participants collaborate in topic-focused tracks, explore use cases, test ideas, develop prototypes, and learn from each other while applying FHIR concepts to Queensland Health priorities. 

The 2026 Connectathon builds on the FHIR Developer Training delivered across Queensland Health over the past two years and supports the growing Queensland Health FHIR Community of Practice.


## Tracks & Collaboration

The 2026 Connectathon will be organised around three broad themes designed to encourage collaboration across multiple disciplines. Participants are encouraged to focus on one area of interest but may move between tracks as required. Many Connectathon activities naturally overlap multiple tracks and benefit from cross-disciplinary 
collaboration.

- FHIR For Services
- FHIR For Data
- FHIR for Users
